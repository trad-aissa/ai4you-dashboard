# Affiliate application kit — ai4you.site

Apply in this order (best payout-to-effort first). When approved, add the program
to the dashboard: slug, label, note, your tracking URL, shelf category, commission.

Site to mention in applications: https://www.ai4you.site
Traffic is early-stage; lead with the editorial angle (sourced AI news + honest
disclosure-first shelf) rather than traffic numbers.

- Angle: "We compare AI writers head-to-head in our monthly scorecards"

## 2. ElevenLabs (in-house program)
- Apply: https://elevenlabs.io/affiliates (or Partners link in footer)
- Angle: voice-AI coverage in video/audio section

## 3. Descript (15-20% recurring, in-house)
